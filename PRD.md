## 1. Vision & Executive Summary

**Purse** is an edge-native, decentralized personal finance copilot designed to liberate individuals from opaque cloud databases and cumbersome financial spreadsheets. Built around **Fiona**, an embodied 3D virtual financial secretary, Purse unites conversational intelligence, local-first zero-knowledge data security, and multi-asset bookkeeping.

Whether a user is unbanked, relies primarily on physical cash, manages Web3 crypto assets across multiple blockchains, or operates traditional high-street checking and savings accounts, Purse provides a unified, private command center.

---

## 2. Core Problem Statement

1. **Privacy Concerns**: Mainstream personal finance aggregators (Mint successors, Plaid-based cloud tools) store sensitive bank transaction logs on remote servers, monetize user spending patterns, and require permanent OAuth linkages.
2. **Exclusion of Cash & Crypto**: Most apps assume every financial movement passes through a domestic checking account or credit card. Users who manage physical cash, privacy-centric petty cash, or self-custody Web3 cryptocurrency are forced to compromise or maintain disjointed spreadsheets.
3. **Rigid AI Chatbots**: Most financial assistants are robotic, deflection-heavy bots that cannot answer simple general knowledge questions (e.g. asking about a university, general advice, or conversational topics) without awkwardly reciting account balances or giving canned error messages.
4. **Dangerous Autonomous Write Access**: Users fear AI hallucinating transactions or modifying accounts without explicit user inspection and approval.

## 3. Product Features & Requirements

### 3.1 Conversational Copilot & Intelligent Persona (Fiona)
- **Generic Knowledge Queries**:
  - When asked general questions (e.g. "Do you know about BRAC University?", "Explain dollar-cost averaging", "What is inflation?"), Fiona must reply conversationally, accurately, and thoroughly using the currently selected LLM.
  - Fiona must never deflect away from general inquiries or force the conversation back to bank balances when the user is asking an external question.
- **Autonomous Decision for Financial Operations**:
  - The model dynamically evaluates whether user intent requires operating on financial data (adding transactions, creating accounts, scheduling bills, or logging memory rules).
  - If financial modification is required, Fiona formulates a structured `action_json` proposal attached to her response.
- **Multi-LLM Selection**:
  - Supports Google Gemini (Default: `gemini-3.8-flash`), OpenAI (`gpt-4o-mini`), DeepSeek (`deepseek-chat`), Zhipu (`glm-4-flash`), and offline Browser Edge LM (`smolLM2-135M-instruct`).
- **3D Interactive Avatar & Voice**:
  - Real-time 3D VRM avatar with Mixamo retargeted idle animations and phoneme-driven procedural lip-sync.
  - Web Speech API integration for real-time speech-to-text (STT) and text-to-speech (TTS) audio narration.

### 3.2 Multi-Asset Account Topology
- **Cash Accounts (Default)**:
  - Users can start from scratch with a **Physical Cash Wallet** as the default account without specifying a bank or financial institution.
  - Cash is treated as liquid capital in liquidity math, runway forecasts, and safe daily spend calculations.
- **Crypto Wallets**:
  - Support for multi-chain digital wallets (Solana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, Polygon, etc.).
  - Fields for public wallet address and network identifier with visual crypto asset badges.
- **Bank & Custodial Accounts**:
  - Checking accounts, High-Yield Savings vaults, Credit cards, and Investment brokerages.
  - Supports institution naming and account number masking (`•••• 1234`).
- **Inter-Account Transfers**:
  - Full support for transfers between accounts (e.g. withdrawing ATM cash from checking into the cash wallet, or transferring fiat to a crypto wallet), updating source and destination balances atomically.

### 3.3 Safe Execution Proposal Gate
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard**:
  - Any mutating action (adding transactions, creating accounts, updating bills, modifying rules) generated by Fiona renders an interactive **Execution Gate Card** in chat.
  - Actions remain in `pending` state until the user clicks **Approve & Execute**.
- **One-Click Reversibility (Rollback Stack)**:
  - Every committed action can be rolled back with one click from the Overview dashboard or action log, reverting balance deltas and ledger entries.

### 3.4 Data Ingestion & Statement Parser
- **Dual Ingestion Engine**:
  - **AI-Powered Extraction**: Accepts CSV exports, bank statements, or messy text notes, structuring them into accounts, categorized transactions, and upcoming bills.
  - **Deterministic Local Parser**: Fallback local regex parser operating completely client-side.
- **Clean Scratch Initialization**:
  - Quick-start pathway creating a default Cash account with zero dependencies.

## 4. User Personas

### Persona 1: Tariq (The Privacy-Conscious Cash & Crypto Earner)
- **Profile**: Freelancer and digital nomad who receives payments in cash and Solana USDC.
- **Pain Point**: Cloud budgeting tools insist on linking a US checking account via Plaid.
- **How Purse Helps**: Tariq initializes Purse with a default Cash Pocket and a Phantom Solana wallet. No cloud credentials required.

### Persona 2: Elena (The Multi-Account Optimizer)
- **Profile**: Software engineer with a primary checking account, Marcus high-yield savings, crypto wallet, and credit cards.
- **Pain Point**: Difficulty keeping track of true liquid runway and knowing whether an unexpected $150 dinner fits today's safe spend.
- **How Purse Helps**: Fiona computes real-time daily safe spend and zero-income survival runway across all liquid accounts.

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

1. **Performance**: In-browser vector search completes within 10ms. Real-time streaming AI chunks render with zero layout shift.
2. **Availability & Offline Resilience**: In offline mode, Browser Edge LM operates via WebGPU/CacheStorage without internet connectivity.
3. **Data Security**: Zero telemetry, zero external user-tracking analytics, and zero server-side transaction logging.
4. **Compatibility**: Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and widescreen desktop displays; PWA compliant.

---

## 6. Success Metrics & KPIs

- **Cognitive Accuracy**: >95% accuracy in distinguishing general knowledge queries from ledger mutations.
- **Execution Safety**: 100% of LLM-initiated financial mutations gated through user confirmation.
- **Time to First Record**: New users can initialize a clean Cash or Crypto ledger in under 30 seconds.
